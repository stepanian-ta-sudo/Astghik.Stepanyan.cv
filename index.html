[ 01 ] About

A filmmaker at the intersection of cinema and the internet.

I'm Astghik Stepanyan — a Moscow-based filmmaker with over five years of experience in editing, writing and shooting.

Currently studying directing at the Moscow School of New Cinema. I approach every project — from a 90-second reel to a feature-length film — with the same cinematic discipline: every cut matters, every frame has intention.

I work in Adobe Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve on a Mac M2. Fast turnaround, clean cuts, considered color.

[ 02 ] Craft

The skills behind the frames.

Six disciplines, one goal — stories that stay with you after the screen goes dark.

/ 01
Editing
Professional cuts, rhythm and pacing. Feature films, podcasts, reels, YouTube shows — storytelling through the timeline.

Directing
Currently studying directing at MSNC. Working on visual narrative, actor work, and cinematic language.
